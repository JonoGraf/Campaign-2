### Description
The Divine Sundering marks the moment the [[the Triad|Triad]] and the greater pantheon of gods severed their direct ties to the [[Material Plane]]. In the wake of [[Orcus]]’ annihilation, [[Tiamat]]’s unmaking, and the near-collapse of [[Mechanus]], the gods concluded that their continued presence invited existential threats. By unanimous decree, divine avatars were withdrawn, planar gates collapsed, and prayers ceased to receive direct answer. The gods aren't dead, they're watching and they won't help.

Mortals were left victorious - yet alone. The Sundering is recorded as both the greatest triumph of mortal will and the beginning of the Age of Absent Gods.

#### The Truths the Gods Never Told
This is what is generally known, however, more learned folk know that [[Orcus]] and [[Tiamat]] didn’t just die, they were part of the cosmic load-bearing structure.

Before their deaths, [[Orcus]] and [[Tiamat]] were anchors.
Not benevolent ones, necessary ones.
They acted as cosmic counterweights, absorbing and stabilizing:
- Excess negative energy ([[Orcus]])
- Excess draconic / elemental / primordial divinity ([[Tiamat]])

When these anchors were lost the gods sealed themselves away to maintain the balance.

Killing both anchors within a short span of cosmic time destabilized the system.  Killing them didn’t break the world everywhere it broke it where the gods were weakest.
