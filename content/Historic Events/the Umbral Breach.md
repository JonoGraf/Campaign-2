### Description
A colossal magical rift of living darkness, called the Umbral Breach, split the land from the rest of Tal’Dorei. It is not just a chasm, it is a boundary. Light bends wrong near it. Sound dies. Spells cast across it unravel.

No army has crossed it in eight centuries.

#### Properties:
- Appears as a miles-wide gulf of slow-moving shadow, like ink suspended in air.
- Sometimes narrows. Sometimes widens. Never predictably.
- Divine magic functions on either side, but cannot cross the Breach.
- Creatures thrown into it are never seen again… but sometimes heard.
- Most scholars believe this was the exact point where the gods sealed themselves away too late, or too forcefully, the equivalent of slamming a door on your own hand.
