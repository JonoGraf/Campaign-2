### PART ONE: THE CONTACT

#### Morning After Session One

The players wake at the Ashveil Inn. Desh puts eggs in front of them without being asked. This is a good sign.

Oren is outside.

Standing across the street from the inn's entrance, still as a fence post, hands folded. A cat from the inn has settled near his feet and gone to sleep. When the players emerge he meets their eyes, nods once, and begins walking north without checking if they follow.

> _"Cult of the Open Silence. They don't speak. Don't take it personally."_

A beat.

> _"They're not dangerous. They're just... committed."_

This is the most diplomatic thing Valkryth has said about the Cult in recent memory, and anyone who knows him would clock it as such.

**If players don't follow Oren:** He stops after twenty yards. Turns. Waits. He will do this twice. On the third stop he produces a card:

> _The Hollowed would speak with you. It concerns the Breach. You will be compensated._

He puts the card away and walks. He will not wait a fourth time — but he will be back tomorrow morning.

---

#### The Still House

The Cult's building near the village's western edge. Windows covered in heavy grey cloth. A door with no outside handle, only a small iron knocker in the shape of an open hand.

Oren knocks twice. A pause. The door opens.

Inside: sparse, clean, cold. A dozen Listeners in various states of meditation — some facing the cloth-covered windows, some simply seated, eyes closed. None acknowledge the players' entrance. The silence is total and immediate.

**WIS DC 11:** The silence here feels slightly thicker than it should. Not magical. More like the room has been practised into quiet over a very long time.

**Valkryth** steps inside with the ease of someone who has done this before — not comfortable exactly, but not rattled. He keeps his voice low instinctively, the way people do in libraries and infirmaries.

**Character moments in the Still House:**

_Andrew_ — a paladin in a room full of people who have renounced divine connection is a specific kind of uncomfortable. He does not make a scene. He is aware of every divine instinct telling him this place is wrong and is actively choosing not to act on it. This costs him something.

_Eva_ — notices the temperature differential between this room and outside. Writes it down. Notices that her compass, when she checks it quietly, points slightly differently in here than it did in the market square. Does not write this down yet. Is thinking about it.

_Sevrian_ — the enforced silence is physically difficult. They are vibrating slightly. They manage. Barely.

Oren leads them to the back room.

---

#### The Hollowed: Davan

A man in his sixties. Former cleric of Avandra, Hollowed for fifteen years. Speaks quietly and with deliberate economy. When he casts, light dims rather than brightens. His healing feels cold.

He gestures for the players to sit. There are exactly enough chairs.

> _"You handled yourselves well yesterday. That matters here more than reputation."_

He looks at Valkryth with the recognition of two people who have been in each other's orbit without ever properly meeting.

> _"And you I know by function, if not by name."_

Valkryth gets a nod. Not warm. Respectful.

Davan explains the situation at Ashmore — the vigil site disturbed, the constant sound, six weeks of lost access, the Cult's inability to handle it themselves.

### PART TWO: THE ROAD TO ASHMORE

#### Three Miles North

The path into the Umbra Hills is a habit worn into shale by forty years of Cult feet. Navigable but uncomfortable. The Breach is visible to the west the entire climb.


**Environmental details:**

The birds stop half a mile before Ashmore. Not suddenly — gradually, like a sound being turned down.

The temperature drops several degrees. Not dramatically. Just enough.

A flat rock on the path carries Cult chalk notation — Breach position records. The most recent entry is two days old. The handwriting is different from all the earlier entries. Shakier. Written fast.

**Perception DC 13:** The chalk on the most recent entry is smeared, sleeve-caught while still wet. Whoever wrote it was moving quickly when they left.

### PART THREE: ASHMORE

#### Approaching the Ruin

Ashmore resolves from the hillside as the players crest the final ridge. A watchtower — four stories originally, the top two collapsed inward, the remainder hunched and thick-walled. Dark grey stone with a faint iridescent quality in certain lights, like oil on water. Pre-Sundering construction. It has the look of a building that survived things by refusing to participate in them.

The Cult's additions: chalk markings on the outer walls, a wooden lean-to, a fire pit unused for six weeks, grey cloth over two lower windows.

**The sound:** Audible from fifty yards. A deep rhythmic grinding — stone on stone — pulsing roughly every eight seconds. Underneath it, barely perceptible, a layered harmonic that sits just below comfortable hearing. It makes the back teeth ache.

**WIS DC 10:** The harmonic feels like it's trying to resolve into something recognisable — a word, almost — but never quite gets there.

**Character reactions to first hearing it:**

_Sevrian_ — stops walking. Their head tilts. They are listening with professional attention. _"That's not random. That's structured. Whatever is making that sound is doing it deliberately."_ They cannot tell if this is more or less alarming than the alternative.

_Eva_ — checks her compass. It is spinning slowly. She counts the pulse interval. Eight seconds. She checks her notes from the meeting with Davan. She looks at the building. _"The Breach moved the night the grinding started. Three weeks before that it moved again. Two weeks before that—"_ She stops. Recounts. _"It's accelerating."_

_Andrew_ — divine instinct. Something in there is not right in a way that has nothing to do with divinity — which is itself the problem. He has trained his whole life to feel the presence of sacred and profane. This is neither. This is something prior to the categories.

_Valkryth_ — professional assessment, immediate: one entry point, two collapsed floors above creating structural instability, unknown creature inside. He positions himself at the entrance and waits for the others to finish their observations before moving in.

---

#### The Dungeon: General Environmental Rules

Three things are wrong with the physics inside Ashmore:

**1. Light bends.** Torchlight and magical light curve slightly toward the centre of whatever room the players are in. Shadows fall at wrong angles. Players always feel slightly off-centre.

**2. Sound layers.** Every sound produces an echo that arrives _before_ the sound, not after. For loud sounds — combat, shouting, spell effects — the pre-echo is clear and disorienting.

**3. The grinding pulse.** Every eight seconds, physical — felt in the feet. During combat, any player concentrating on a spell when the pulse hits makes DC 10 CON save or loses concentration.

---

#### Room One: The Entry Hall

Once a guard room. Stone floor, two collapsed benches, a staircase going up, a doorway going deeper. Cult grey cloth over one window.

**Encounter: 12x Breach-Touched Stirges**

Modified: when they attach and feed, the target hears a faint whisper in a voice they almost recognise. Not words. Just tone — loving, or warning. It lasts only as long as the stirge feeds. It does no additional mechanical damage. It is purely, specifically unsettling.

**Character-specific stirge moments:**

_Andrew_ — the voice he hears during the feed sounds, briefly, like it might be divine. It is not. But it is the closest thing to a divine communication he has felt since arriving in Jorenn and the fact that it comes from a parasite feeding on his blood is information he will need time to process.

_Yoa_ — one stirge bypasses the group and goes directly for her. It is drawn to something — warmth, movement, scent. She deals with it efficiently. The voice she hears is a child's. She does not mention this to the others.

_Xylira_ — the whisper she hears is in a language she doesn't recognise. It is not Elvish. It is not Undercommon. It is not anything she can place. She files this away.

**Investigation DC 11 on the scratched bench:** The marks were made by something with an irregular number of limbs. The pattern doesn't match any standard creature gait.

#### Room Two: The Observation Chamber
TRAP --
LOOT -- Potion of Comprehension and Spell Scroll (lvl 2) and oil of etherealness

Wide circular room, narrow windows at intervals giving sightlines across the valley. The Breach is visible through the western windows, closer here than from town. The Cult's vigil station: low cushions, chalk notation on the floor, a locked wooden chest.

**No combat. Information and atmosphere.**

**The chest** (locked, DC 13 Thieves' Tools or DC 15 STR): Thirty years of Cult observation records — the promised payment. Davan gave no one the key. He assumed they would find a way.

**Eva and the records:** If Eva examines them she can parse the key patterns with DC 12 Investigation. The Breach has been accelerating its shifts for approximately three years. The rate doubled in the last six months. She fills two pages of her field journal with cross-referenced data and looks up with the expression of someone who has confirmed something they were hoping was wrong.

**The western windows:** From this elevation the Breach doesn't just sit — it moves. Slowly, like breathing. In. Out. In. Out.

**Perception DC 14:** The rhythm of the Breach matches the grinding pulse from deeper in the ruin. Almost exactly.

**Sevrian and the chalk notation:** The Cult's floor markings are not decorative — they are a recorded language of Breach observation. With DC 13 Investigation, Sevrian recognises enough structural patterns to realise this is a form of musical notation adapted for non-musical recording. The Cult has been transcribing the Breach's movements as if it were a composition. _"It has a tempo,"_ Sevrian says quietly. _"Someone has been writing it down."_

---

#### Room Three: The Lower Passage

Narrow corridor, low ceiling, walls dense with Cult chalk markings. The pre-echo phenomenon is strongest here. Players hear their own footsteps before they take them — clearly, unmistakably. A player who stops walking hears two more footsteps arrive after they stop.

**The puzzle mechanic — three chalk circles:**

_First circle:_ Pre-echo reverses — sounds arrive slightly late. Normal.

_Second circle:_ Sound stops entirely for the person standing in it. Total silence, even as others speak. Disorienting, not harmful.

_Third circle:_ The grinding pulse becomes a clear tone — almost musical. A low D.

**Sevrian** — recognises the note immediately, unprompted, the moment they stand in the third circle. _"That's a D. Low D, specifically."_ They look at the walls. At the Cult's notation. _"They've been mapping this."_ They look at the direction of the vault. _"Whatever is in there is singing. It just doesn't know it."_

**Valkryth** — stands in the second circle and experiences total silence for the first time since the grinding started. He stands there for longer than is strictly necessary. When he steps out he says nothing. But he rolls his shoulders, once, like a man who has just put down something heavy.

---

#### Room Four: The Vault — Boss Encounter

Twenty feet across, domed ceiling, floor lower than the corridor by three steps.

**6x Darklings** — fast, coordinating, light-hungry, averse to being observed directly. They move through wrong-angled shadows, repositioning when watched.

**The Ashmore Resonator** — at the centre of the vault. A perfectly spherical stone, eighteen inches in diameter, floating eight inches off the floor. Ancient. Pre-Sundering. Activated by the Breach's increasing proximity. The Darklings cluster around it, using its wrong-light aura for cover. While adjacent they have half cover.

**Combat notes by character:**

_Valkryth_ — the Darklings' shadow-movement is exactly the kind of threat his guard training did not prepare him for. He adapts by anchoring — picking one position, forcing them to come to him rather than chasing them through the shadows. It works. It is not elegant. It is effective.

_Yoa_ — professionally at home in the wrong light. The pre-echo means she hears her own movements before she makes them. She uses this. DC 13 Perception check to use the pre-echo offensively — she knows where her strike lands a half-second before it does. Advantage on one attack per round if she succeeds.

_Sevrian_ — the Resonator's pulse threatens concentration spells. Every eight seconds. Sevrian is aware of this because they counted in the corridor. They time their casting around the pulse with DC 12 Performance — treating the rhythm like a musical beat, casting in the window between pulses. Success means no concentration checks this combat.

_Eva_ — the Resonator is the most significant magical object she has encountered in her nascent field career. She is aware that the correct academic response is documentation. She is aware that there are Darklings trying to kill her. She manages both, imperfectly.

_Andrew_ — the Darklings are feeding on diverted light. His divine light-producing abilities make him a target. Every spell that produces light draws two Darklings immediately. He learns this after the first casting. He adjusts.

_Xylira_ — her Djinni patron's influence — air, movement, the particular quality of directed wind — is useful in the enclosed vault. She can use air-based attacks to force Darklings out of shadow cover without needing to see them clearly. The wind in the vault moves toward her, just slightly, the whole fight.

**The puzzle — deactivating the Resonator:**

Killing the Darklings doesn't stop it. The ceiling is cracking — nine pulses before active debris falls (DC 10 DEX save each pulse, 1d4 bludgeoning).

End of the Resonators Turn - flag some players that they can feel the vibrations - if they dont move they get bonked at the start of the resonators turn

**The solution:** Produce the low D note clearly, adjacent to the Resonator.

**How each character might solve it:**

_Sevrian_ — automatic. They have the note, they have the instrument, they have the timing from the corridor. They can do this mid-combat if the others give them ten seconds of cover.

_Eva_ — DC 13 Arcana to modulate a cantrip into the correct frequency. Prestidigitation producing a specific tone is automatic if she thought to bring the note from the corridor observation.

_Valkryth_ — DC 14 Athletics to strike the Resonator at the correct angle with a blunt weapon. He will not enjoy being told to hit a magic rock in a specific way. He will do it.

_Xylira_ — a precisely directed gust of wind through a narrow opening — her cloak, a bottle neck, her own cupped hands — can produce a tone. DC 13 Arcana to get the frequency right.

_Andrew_ — DC 13 Religion. He has encountered resonance-based sacred architecture before. Some pre-Sundering shrines used tonal locks. This is the same principle.

_Yoa_ — DC 14 Sleight of Hand to find the strike angle through instinct and precision rather than knowledge.

**When the Resonator deactivates:**

The grinding stops. The pre-echo fades immediately. Light straightens. Shadows fall correctly. The silence that follows is the good kind — natural, earned.

The Darklings scatter. Two flee through wall cracks. Two are cornered and finished.

The Resonator drops to the floor. Smooth, warm, twelve pounds. Faintly iridescent. Clearly significant. Clearly not from anywhere recent.

### PART FOUR: THE RETURN

#### Back to Davan

Davan listens to the account without interrupting.

> _"The Resonator is not ours. We didn't place it. If it was active, that means the Breach has been feeding it."_

He looks at it — or at the players, if they kept it.

> _"The Breach is getting louder. This is what that sounds like when it has something to vibrate against."_

He pays without complaint. If they kept the Resonator, Oren watches it with fixed attention as they leave. Davan does not ask for it. He does not need to.


---

#### The Cult Records

Handed over in a wrapped cloth bundle. Thirty years of Breach observations — dry, thorough, and deeply unsettling in aggregate. A player who spends an hour with them and makes DC 13 Investigation identifies the inflection point: three years ago, the Breach's behaviour changed. It began moving with more regularity. Less like a wound. More like something breathing.

### PART FIVE: HARVEN — THE CLOSING IMAGE

#### That Evening

The players are back in Jorenn. Desh puts food in front of them without being asked. Good food — she has decided she likes them. The fire is working. The Resonator, if they kept it, hums faintly when Valkryth walks past it. He moves to the other side of the table.

Harven finds them.

He looks different from session one. Not distressed — unmoored. He is carrying his sketchbook pressed against his chest with both hands. He sits without being invited. He opens to a new page and sets it on the table.

> _"I drew this today. This afternoon. I remember drawing it. I remember the light coming through my window, I remember the charcoal, I remember finishing it."_

A pause.

> _"I don't remember deciding to draw it."_

**The new sketch:**

The party — all six, rendered accurately. A street in Jorenn, near the market. An ordinary scene they were not present for today because they were at Ashmore.

Standing with them — close, natural, part of the group — is the seventh figure. The same young man from session one.

But this time Harven has drawn him twice. Once in the group. Once in the background, half-visible around a corner, watching the group from a distance.

The same figure. In two places. In a sketch Harven does not remember deciding to draw. Of a scene that did not happen.

> _"I think,"_ Harven says carefully, _"I think I know him."_

He touches the figure's face in the sketch. Doesn't press. Just touches.

> _"I can't remember from where."_

He closes the sketchbook. Stands. Gives them a small uncertain smile that doesn't reach his eyes.

> _"Probably nothing."_

He leaves.
