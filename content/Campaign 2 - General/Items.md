### Metamagic Gems
- Voker's gems grant advantage on the attack roll for an Evocation spell with a single target, and if the result is a natural 20, the target is knocked away 15 feet.
- Viner's gems grant advantage on the next initiative roll before a long rest.
- Juror's gems grant advantage on the next saving throw.
- Muter's gems cause a creature affected by a Transmutation spell to have their speed changed in some way (acquiring a climbing or swimming speed equal to the walking speed, a flying speed equal to half the walking speed, or losing half of their walking speed)
