### Character Creation
- 2024/5.5e
	- Source books & homebrew
	- Can use old version incl UA/homebrew and will adjust
- Point Buy (27), but then can -2 lowest stat to +1 a stat
- Every PC has a “keepsake”, can be anything; eg. weapon, armour, trinket, focus etc.
	- This is attuned to the PC on a free slot
	- It will upgrade/grow/change with the PC as they do cool stuff/learn and mechanically add abilities/buffs to character 

### World
- Same world as campaign 1 but different continent (using Tal’Dorei as map base)
- 1000 years later
- Campaign 1 characters existed in this world but are now more legend/stories
	- Possibility for references but unlikely interaction
- Slightly ‘lower magic/divine’ vibe than Eberron especially initially
	- Potions/items harder to come by
	- Religion/Magic less pronounced
- Still same other planes etc
