### Homebrew Rules
#### Shields
Shield users can use reaction to make an attack roll against an incoming attack, if higher then the damage is done to the shield durability instead of PC

#### Potions
Potions drunk using an action use the maximum roll

#### Counterspell - tbc
Tossing up idea that Counterspell is done as a contested roll. Will probably start with the new 5.5e RAW and see how it feels

#### Unconscious/Death Changes
- 1pt of exhaustion whenever knocked unconscious (1pt recovered per LR, 2pt recovered if ‘comfortable’)
- Help action can be used to assist on another players death saving throws

#### Resurrection
Since [[the Divine Sundering]] resurrection magic hasn’t worked quite as consistently:
- There is a DC 5 check on any resurrection magic
- Every time a character dies this DC is permanently increased by 5
- The DC is also increased by a failed resurrection attempt
- There are some magical items/rewards to decrease this DC or down time activities could be done to increase likelihood of success

#### Desperate Measures (tbc):
When bloodied (1/2 health) you can push your body to its limit and pre-spend death saving throws (cost) in order to perform one of the following on your turn (resets LR):
- immediately take the dash and disengage as a free action (1)
- +5 bonus to a d20 test you just failed (1)
- Reroll an attack roll that missed (1)
- When you've hit with an attack, instead of rolling for damage deal the maximum die damage (2)
- regain a level 1-5 spell slot (3)
- immediately take the attack/magic action as an extra action (3). Still limited to 1 spell per turn

#### Battlefield Actions
Battlefield actions consist of two phases:
- A Tell, (end of monsters turn) where the Battlefield Action starts. The Tell does not inflict any damage. Instead, it is a hint for the player as to what the Battlefield Action will do once it’s resolved and how to stop it.
- A Resolution, (start of monsters turn) when the Battlefield Action ends. If the players have not neutralized or mitigated the Battlefield Action, the actual effect of this action will resolve in this phase.
- All Battlefield Actions can be either neutralized or mitigated. The point of the Tell phase is for the players to figure out how to neutralize or mitigate the action before it reaches the Resolution stage. If the players don’t manage to neutralize or mitigate a Battlefield Action, the Battlefield Action simply triggers.
