### Description
Avernus was the first layer of the Nine Hells. The most likely beachhead for any attack by demon-kind,  it was the primary battleground of the Blood War: legions of devils marched across its plains in continual readiness to repel the hordes of demon invaders that sailed the River Styx into the layer.
