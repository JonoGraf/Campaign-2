### Description
Jorenn Village is a large village in close proximity to the Umbra Hills in the Black Valley north of Westruun in the east part of the Cliffkeep Mountains.
Leading into the village was the Blackvalley Path that runs along the edge of the [[Bramblewood Forest]] closer to [[Westruun]] but this access has been cut off by [[the Umbral Breach]] so now the only access is a dangerous mountainous path through the [[Umbra Hills]].

Jorenn is far from [[Emon]], and so, despite being a part of the [[Republic of Tal'Dorei]], has often fallen prey to those taking advantage of the lack of influence the Tal'Dorei Council holds there.

For many years, Jorenn Village was a tiny pioneer town, relying primarily on trade of shadegrass from the nearby [[Umbra Hills]]. However, when silver veins were discovered and Jorenn Village became something of a boom town. Now [[the Umbral Breach|The Breach]] is the main point of interest.

#### **Reeve Alric Morn** — Village Leader

Overworked, rational, clinging to order. Projects calm authority while privately exhausted and sleeping poorly. Pushing for tighter mining records and stricter Breach monitoring. Has taken on his brother's administrative duties without knowing why. Two coats hang behind his chair. An unused office sits empty in the town hall. If shown Harven's sketch he stares at the extra figure longer than anyone else, rubs his temple, and moves on. _Hydra victim: Edrin Morn — his younger brother, who managed the mining ledgers._

#### **Foreman Brakka Stonevein** — Head Miner

Dwarf woman, fifties, loud and blunt. Believes problems are physical and solvable with tools. Runs Shaft Three with iron precision — except her numbers don't add up and she becomes visibly irritated when anyone points it out. She doesn't know why she's angry. There is an extra helmet in the mine, a pickaxe initialled T.V., and a locker nobody opens. _Hydra victim: Tolan Veck — a young, ambitious miner._

#### **Sister Kaelin Voss** — Travelling Cleric

Soft-spoken, observant, stationed temporarily in Jorenn. Has noticed fewer funerals than expected, fewer births than recorded, and a hymn that feels off. Wears a ring she cannot explain owning. Keeps a second pillow in her quarters. Feels grief she cannot name or source. Of all the NPCs she is the most likely to sense the cracks in reality — her divine sensitivity picks up the Hydra's residue as a persistent, sourceless sorrow. _Hydra victim: Jessa Voss — her wife, who ran a small herbal stall._

#### **Harven Dell** — The Artist

Gentle, curious, quietly observant. Documents significant events in charcoal for the village record. Genuinely believes he draws only what he sees — and is beginning to be frightened by what that implies. His hand remembers what his mind cannot hold. Produces sketches he doesn't remember deciding to draw. Has chalk marks at child-height on his walls. A small pair of boots by the door. A half-finished drawing of a child he cannot explain starting. _Hydra victim: Mira Dell — his daughter, age eight._

#### **Captain Rul Hendrik** — Security Commander

Lean, precise, disciplined. Tracks everyone who enters and leaves Jorenn in a small leather ledger he keeps on his person. His records are immaculate — and consistently wrong in ways he cannot locate. Keeps rechecking numbers that don't resolve. An extra spear in the rack. An armour set that fits no one. Seven beds in a six-man barracks. Feels like he's missing something tactical and cannot identify what. _Hydra victim: Garron Holt — a guard._

#### **Desh** — Innkeeper, Ashveil Inn

Half-orc, forties, shaved head. Runs the Ashveil Inn with low tolerance for nonsense and genuine warmth for guests she decides she likes. When Yoa walks in something happens to her face she cannot account for — a loosening, a reaching toward a shape that isn't there. Has been running the inn without a bouncer for weeks and hasn't thought to hire one. Keeps starting letters she doesn't know who to address. _Hydra victim: Clothesline — a Tabaxi fighter, her old friend, working the door._

#### **Oren** — Listener, Cult of the Open Silence

Late twenties, plain clothes, completely silent. Carries pre-written cards for interactions with strangers. Was present near the mine before the collapse, unsurprised by the creature attack, and watching the town hall from the street. Not a threat — a devoted initiate cataloguing outsiders and monitoring official response to Breach events. His presence across three incidents is suspicious and entirely explainable. That is the point.

#### **Davan** — Hollowed, Cult of the Open Silence

Sixties, former cleric of Avandra, underwent the Unbinding fifteen years ago. Still has access to his spells — they simply manifest wrongly. Healing feels cold. Light dims when he casts. Speaks quietly and deliberately. The Cult's primary point of contact for the players in session two. Pragmatic, not warm. Will deal fairly.

#### **Sable** — Unvoiced, Cult of the Open Silence

Late forties. Entered the Breach partially during her Unvoiced rite twelve years ago and returned without her voice — not damaged, simply absent. Communicates via a worn chalkboard. The sharpest mind in Jorenn and the most dangerous Cult member — not through violence but through clear sight. Beginning to suspect the silence has a direction. Watching the players carefully. Will communicate first through unsigned written notes. Her tell: when genuinely moved or frightened she opens her mouth and nothing comes out.

#### **Petra** — Deaf Child
Eight years old. Born deaf. Best friend of Mira Dell. The only witness to the False Hydra because the song cannot reach her. Has been trying to tell someone what she saw for two weeks. Communicates through chalk drawings and gesture. Patient with people who are patient with her. Has stopped going to Harven because he keeps forgetting their conversations. Knows exactly what is under the town. Cannot make anyone understand it. _Not a Hydra victim — her deafness makes her immune to its song._
