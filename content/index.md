# Preamble
Nine hundred years ago, your [[Cantrips Pty Ltd|predecessors]] saved the world.
You know the stories. Everyone does. The heroes who brought down [[Orcus]], who unmade [[Tiamat]], who held [[Mechanus]] together with mortal hands. [[The Triad]] witnessed it and when it was over, the gods looked at what mortals had accomplished and made a decision.

They left.
Not in defeat. Not in anger. By choice, and by unanimous decree, the divine withdrew. Avatars dissolved. Planar gates collapsed. Prayers still rise they simply no longer receive answer. The gods are watching. They are simply done intervening. What mortals could do, mortals would do. The Age of Absent Gods began on the day of [[the Divine Sundering]], and it has not ended since.

The world did not end. It adapted. Faith survived, quieter, harder, built on conviction rather than miracle. Magic persisted, rarer now, less ambient, more deliberate. Civilisations rebuilt on mortal will alone and found, to their occasional surprise, that mortal will was sufficient.

But the Sundering left a scar.
No one fully understands it. The scholars of [[the Cobalt Soul]] have theories. [[The Ashari]] have older ones. What is agreed upon is this: when [[Orcus]] and [[Tiamat]] died, something structural died with them. Something that had been holding a tension in the fabric of the world released all at once and the world tore.

[[The Umbral Breach]] opened in the Black Valley, north of Westruun, in the shadow of the [[Cliffkeep Mountains]]. Miles wide. A gulf of slow-moving darkness, like ink suspended in the air, where light bends wrong and sound dies and spells unravel at the edge. It does not stay still. It breathes. It narrows and widens without pattern or warning. Things thrown into it are never seen again though sometimes, in the deep hours of the night, they are heard.

The Blackvalley Path is gone, swallowed in the first years after the Sundering, cutting off the eastern settlements from the rest of [[Tal'Dorei]]. The only way in or out now is the mountain pass through the Umbra Hills. At the edge of the Breach sits [[Jorenn Village]].

It was a pioneer town once. Shadegrass and thin soil and people stubborn enough to call it home. Then silver was found in the mountain rock, and Jorenn became something — a boom town, a destination, a place worth the difficult journey through the pass. It is still that, technically. The mine still runs. The market still trades. The [[Tal'Dorei Council]]'s authority still technically extends this far, though Jorenn has learned not to wait for it.

You have each come here for your own reasons but you are here.
The Breach is visible to the west, if you care to look. Most people have stopped looking.
It doesn't seem to mind.
