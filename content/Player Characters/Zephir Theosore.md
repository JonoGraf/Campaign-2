### Backstory
Zephir was the youngest provisional researcher ever accepted into [[the Arcana Pansophical]]'s field assessment program. Unfortunately, she misunderstood what that meant.

While older students saw admission as the beginning of their education, Eva saw it as proof that she was already exceptional. She became the sort of person who:
* Corrects senior researchers on technical terminology.
* Uses unnecessarily formal language.
* Refers to ordinary conversations as "informal interviews."
* Treats every experience as data collection.
* Secretly believes most people are intellectually lazy.

She prides herself on being rational and objective, but her emotional life is a disaster.
When criticized, she becomes defensive.
When ignored, she becomes resentful.
When proven wrong, she immediately starts searching for a way she was technically right.

She genuinely believes she is more mature than people her age because she spends her time studying magical theory instead of socializing. The irony is that everyone older can see she's still a teenager. 

<img src="Zephir Theosore Library.png" style="display: block; margin-left: auto; margin-right: auto; text-align: center;" width="80%">

Since then she has spent years cultivating the demeaner of a respected academic: precise speech, meticulous notes, professional distance, and an intolerance for frivolity. She privately regards most people her own age as unserious and most adults as disappointingly imprecise thinkers. Her supervisors regard her as brilliant, promising, hardworking, and occasionally exhausting. Zephir believes this assignment will be the report that establishes her reputation. She has not yet considered the possibility that the Breach may be studying her as thoroughly as she is studying it.

She’s determined that she will be the youngest arch wizard in history. She grew up in a family of free spirted local nobles, who enjoyed poetry, dances and lively conversation. They think of her fondly, but with confusion- she thinks of them as intellectually lazy layabouts. She still attends family functions when required, but will bring a book.
