### Backstory
<img src="Yoa.jpg" style="float: right; margin-left: 20px;" width="275">
Yoa is a Tabaxi, from a loving family that run a successful merchant company. Her family want her to go to business school so she can start to take over the family business, but Yoa has always dreamt of being a ninja like her great grandmother, [[Yoru]]. Her parents tell her she can have a gap year to get this ninja dream out of her head, before starting her studies. 

Starting young, Yoa is an expert in various martial arts, is a great climber and after working at her parents shop, she is a great negotiator (around other Tabaxi) and craftsperson.
