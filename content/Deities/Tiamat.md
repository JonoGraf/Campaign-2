### Description
Tiamat was the lawful evil goddess of greed, queen of chromatic dragons and, for a time, reluctant servant of the greater gods Bane and later [[Asmodeus]]

Tiamat was also the eternal rival of her brother [[Bahamut]], ruler of the good metallic dragons. Despite their current enmity, legends held that they once worked together to create a planet known as the First World, which laid the foundation for the forging of the [[Material Plane]]]

### Defeat
Tiamat has had many defeats and victories of the Millenia but notably her recent defeat by the [[Cantrips Pty Ltd|Cantrips]] led to [[the Divine Sundering]].

Tiamat wanted to use the essences of [[Orcus]] and Zariel to remould the [[Material Plane]] in her visage as the Queen of Death and Flame. After the climatic battle in [[Avernus]] her divine essence was absorbed by the victorious [[Cantrips Pty Ltd]] becoming lesser deities themselves.
