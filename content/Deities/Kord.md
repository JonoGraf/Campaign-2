# Kord, the Stormlord
![[Kord.png]]

### Description
Kord, the Stormlord, is the storm god and the lord of battle and warfare. He is worshiped by the denizens of the Braving Grounds district of Vasselheim and sailors of the [[Menagerie Coast]] in [[Wildemount]].

His worship is banned in [[the Dwendalian Empire]].

#### Commandments of the Stormlord
- _Bravery above all. There is no glory in cowardice._
- _Strength is the path to greatness, but greatness is the responsible use of strength._
- _The glory of the Stormlord lives through your own glory on the battlefield._
