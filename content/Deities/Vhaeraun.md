# Vhaeraun, the Masked Lord

### Description
Vhaeraun,  also known as the Masked Lord and the Masked God of Night, is the drow god of thievery
He held the unique view among drow that males and females were equally valuable and was primarily prayed to by those drow males who sought a better life than slavery under Lolth's matriarchy and who opposed it.
