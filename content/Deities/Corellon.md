# Corellon, the Arch Heart
![[Corellon.png]]

### Description
Corellon, the Arch Heart is the deity of arcane magic and the fey. During the Founding, the Arch Heart wandered the lands, spreading magic and raising the forests. The Arch Heart created the first elves, and so they are considered the Mother and Father of all elves. Centaurs believe the Arch Heart to be their creator, as well.

There are rumours that Corellon has been seen in the newly formed [[Feywild]] after [[the Divine Sundering]], but this is likely to be wishful thinking from their followers.

#### Commandments of the Arch Heart
- _Create, inspire, and find beauty in all that you do._
- _Follow the echoes of lost magic, forgotten sites, and ancient art, for within these lie the Arch Heart's first works._
- _Combat the followers of the Spider Queen wherever they may be._
