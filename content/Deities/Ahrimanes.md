# Ahrimanes, the Wanderer
![[Ahrimanes.png]]
### Description
Ahrimanes was a demon lord titled the Chief of the Cacodaemons who presided over the 452nd layer of [[the Abyss]], Ahriman-abad.

Ahrimanes was the patron of exiles and wanderers

At some point during the mid–14th century DR, Ahrimanes' physical form could be found within an iron fortress in [[the Abyss]]. During that time was believed to have been traveling across via the [[Astral Plane]]
