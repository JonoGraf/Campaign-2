# Bahamut, the Platinum Dragon
![[Bahamut.png]]

### Description
Bahamut, the Platinum Dragon is the deity of good-aligned dragons and metallic dragons, being considered the first of their kind. He is a sworn enemy of [[Tiamat]], the Scaled Tyrant, who is the queen of the chromatic dragons.

#### Commandments of the Platinum Dragon
- _Stand as a paragon of honor and justice._
- _Smite evil wherever it is found, yet show compassion to those who have strayed from righteousness._
- _Defend the weak, bring freedom to the persecuted, and protect the ideals of justice and order._
