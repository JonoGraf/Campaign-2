### The Cult of the Open Silence
#### Core Beliefs
- [[the Umbral Breach|The Breach]] is not a wound.
- It is the first honest thing the gods ever made.
- The gods ruled by voice, command, and decree.
- [[the Umbral Breach|The Breach]] offers silence and in that silence, truth.

#### Hierarchy
- **Listeners** are initiates trained to meditate near [[the Umbral Breach|the Breach]]. Listeners do not speak out loud
- **Hollowed** are members who have survived direct exposure
- **The Unvoiced** are Leaders who have entered [[the Umbral Breach|the Breach]] partially and returned
- No “high priest” and there is no singular prophet

#### Rituals 
###### The Vigil of Still Breath
Members sit in absolute silence for an hour facing [[the Umbral Breach|the Breach]].
No spells.
No magic items.
No speech.

Those who hear nothing are praised.
Those who hear whispers are watched carefully.
Those who hear their own name…are fast-tracked.

###### The Unbinding
A voluntary rite where a cleric or paladin:
- Renounces their god symbolically
- Attempts to cast a spell in [[the Umbral Breach|the Breach]]’s shadow
- If successful, they become Hollowed

The spell manifests strangely:
- Healing feels cold
- Light emits shadow
- Divine smites leave no scorch marks

###### The Quiet Naming
Children born near [[the Umbral Breach|the Breach]] are given a second name no one ever speaks aloud.
The cult believes [[the Umbral Breach|the Breach]] already knows it.


#### Known Powers/Abilities
Most cult members can:
- Suppress divine auras
- Walk closer to [[the Umbral Breach|the Breach]] without harm
- Detect “false divinity” (relics, avatars, angels)

Higher ranked Silents:
- Cannot be targeted by commune
- Leave no afterlife trace when they die
- Occasionally cause divine magic to hesitate around them
